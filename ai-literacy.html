02 · Reflection

Reflection

Four questions about how AI helped me and where it fell short.

What did AI help me accomplish?

AI helped me move faster on work I understood in outline but had not built before: planning the site structure, writing first drafts of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, explaining unfamiliar features, and spotting a missing tag in my project list. It also helped me write clearer descriptions of my projects.

What did I personally verify?

I opened the site in my browser after each change and clicked through the pages, links, and buttons. I compared names, dates, schools, and certificates with my own documents, and I decided which personal details should stay private. When something looked wrong, like a blank page, I showed what I saw instead of accepting the first answer.

What did I learn from using AI?

Clear, specific requests with screenshots and exact text gave much better results than vague ones. I learned to read the code AI wrote, change values myself, and make small edits instead of replacing whole files. AI is a good tutor for explaining why something works, as long as I test the answer.

What limitations or errors did I discover?

  • AI cannot know my personal facts. It noticed different email addresses in my files, but I had to decide which one is correct.
  • It cannot see my live site unless I show it, so it could not tell what I was seeing when the page was blank.
  • Some suggestions only work in the right setting. A localhost link works on my computer but not for visitors, and PHP does not run on GitHub Pages.
  • AI text can sound confident and still be generic, so I rewrite drafts and placeholders to match my real experience.

03 · Principles

Principles I follow

  • I treat AI output as a first draft and test it before I use it.
  • I check facts, names, and dates against my own records.
  • I decide which personal information goes on public pages.
  • I aim to understand every part of my work well enough to explain it in my own words.
